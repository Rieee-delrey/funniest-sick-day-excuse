One line, under 200 characters, giving a funny reason to call in sick — a reason a boss might half believe and a coworker would laugh at, with the twist landing at the end.

Entry (134 characters):

> I got food poisoning from my own cooking, which means I am both the victim and the prime suspect, so I need the day to solve the case.

`main.py` writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`, reads it back to verify the bytes, and asserts the 1–200 character gate.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty "The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse" ($2, top 3).
